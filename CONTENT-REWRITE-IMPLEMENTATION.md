# Sutra Health — Content Rewrite Implementation

## Purpose
This pass rewrites the existing site copy for clearer search intent, stronger patient understanding, less repetition and safer healthcare wording. It preserves existing routes, components and functionality.

## Content hierarchy
- Homepage: what Sutra Health is and where to begin.
- Conditions: health-concern information.
- Services: what each service is for.
- Individual services: scope, practical questions, process and limitations.
- Approach: how care conversations are structured.
- Doctor: credentials and professional context.
- Assessment: reflective lifestyle check-in, not a diagnosis.
- Patient stories: only verified/consented accounts should be published.
- Resources: search-led educational questions.
- Booking: conversion and appointment information only.

## Implemented in this pass
1. Homepage hero aligned with the approved content direction: “Your partner in recovery and wellness.”
2. Homepage intro now uses the approved medical-guidance + nutrition + everyday-practices + Therapeutic Yoga positioning.
3. Homepage section copy is differentiated so lifestyle, conditions, patient experiences, FAQs and booking each have a separate job.
4. Services landing metadata and headings were clarified.
5. Lifestyle Medicine copy was tightened around six lifestyle areas without implying that all areas must change.
6. Nutrition copy was differentiated around real meals, dietary routines and practical food questions rather than generic wellness language.
7. Physician Consultation copy was tightened around symptoms, history, reports and next steps.
8. Outdated ₹100 consultation pricing language was removed from the physician page.
9. Therapeutic Yoga copy was made more specific about adapted movement/breathing and suitability.
10. Behaviour & Stress copy avoids presenting the service as psychotherapy, psychiatric care or crisis treatment.
11. Approach copy now describes the decision/conversation framework rather than repeating lifestyle-service messaging.
12. FAQ and booking copy was made more task-oriented.

## Editorial guardrails
- Do not invent patient outcomes, statistics, testimonials, credentials or treatment guarantees.
- Verify clinician credentials and professional roles before publication.
- Verify testimonial consent, attribution and exact wording before publication.
- Keep medical disclaimers where clinical decisions are discussed.
- Do not describe the 21-point assessment as diagnostic or validated unless evidence is supplied.
- Do not imply lifestyle support replaces prescribed medical care.
